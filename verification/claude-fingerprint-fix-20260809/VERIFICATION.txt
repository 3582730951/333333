CAUSES CONFIRMED IN THE BASELINE

1. The Claude identity tuple was stale (2.1.220) and mixed incompatible version/build axes.
2. encoding/json map serialization exposed sorted Go key order and HTML-safe escaping.
3. claude-cli headers could ride a Chrome/Go ClientHello or HTTP/2 connection rather than native Bun HTTP/1.1.
4. header order/case, Accept-Encoding, beta order, billing text, SDK identity, metadata.user_id, probes, prewarms, custom bridges, and count-token calls could diverge from one another.
5. OAuth login/refresh used old endpoints and non-native headers/body shape; unresolved egress could fall back to the relay host.
6. The external sidecar could inject Chrome headers, reorder headers, strip Connection, or negotiate h2 under a Claude identity.
